A file's stated origin is a claim, not a fact. When you receive a collection, any "from" attribution it carries was written by whoever made the file. Nothing verifies it. Treat received files with the care you would treat any attachment.

6. Ending your use

You may stop using GeneFox at any time, and delete your account from within the app. Deleting removes the account record, its keys and its encrypted records; deletions propagate to your other devices. Signing out happens on each device itself — this version has no way to sign a device out remotely, and deleting the account is what revokes everything at once.

Two things persist deliberately, and you should know about both. Deletion markers are kept for 180 days so that a device which has been offline cannot reintroduce records you deleted. And if you unsubscribed from product-update email, we retain a one-way hash of that address — with no account identifier — so the unsubscribe survives the deletion. It cannot be used to contact you; it exists so that a later list import cannot start mailing you again.

Export before you delete. Because your records are encrypted with a key we do not hold, we cannot recover them for you afterwards.

We may suspend or terminate an account that is being used to break these Terms, to abuse the service, or in a way that endangers other users. Where it is reasonable to do so we will say why.

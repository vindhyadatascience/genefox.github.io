Command-line tool Beta

macOS and Linux

curl -fsSL https://genefox.app/cli/install.sh | bash

Installs to ~/.local

Windows

irm https://genefox.app/cli/install.ps1 | iex

Installs to %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\GeneFox

The installer checks the minisign signature and SHA-256.

Verify by hand
minisign -V -P RWTc+F0bd3qlNlApONI2qRpbBAcrDfUhxdVUrsoaVYGdkaHjUWAB75BL -m SHA256SUMS -x SHA256SUMS.minisig

Get SHA256SUMS and SHA256SUMS.minisig from https://storage.googleapis.com/vds-genefox-releases/genefox-cli/<version>/

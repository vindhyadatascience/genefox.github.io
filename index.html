What you can do

A full analysis bench, built for the phone in your pocket.

Cohort exploration, publication-style figures, and rigorous statistics — no cluster, no notebook, no upload of your questions required.

Expression

Stratified expression, five ways to see it

Plot any cohort as box, bar, strip, or violin — split by clinical stage, subtype, sample type, or mutation status, with effect sizes and Mann–Whitney statistics.

Survival

Kaplan–Meier, with the math to back it

Stratify survival by clinical variable, expression, or mutation. Log-rank p-values, median survival, and per-cohort pan-cancer drill-down are one tap away.

Differential

Volcano plots with FDR control

Expression or mutant-vs-wild-type contrasts with panel-wise Benjamini–Hochberg correction.

Heatmap

Clustered gene-panel heatmaps

Hierarchical clustering, multi-track annotations, Z-normalization, and an outlier-resistant diverging scale.

Signature

Custom signature scoring

Score any cohort against a gene signature — histogram, statistics, and signature-split survival.

Cohorts

100+ GDC cohorts

Case counts, sites, disease types, and demographics — with a Summary tab for at-a-glance context.

GEO

40,000+ GEO datasets, triaged

Search NCBI GEO with smart cancer-term expansion — over 40,000 Series come pre-verified to actually have usable expression data, spanning microarray, supplementary matrices, and ARCHS4 (human & mouse) RNA-seq.

Reports

PDF & HTML reports

Capture any figure into the Report Builder and export multi-figure reports with an auto-generated methods section.

Private by construction

Your questions stay yours.

GeneFox has no ads, no behavioral analytics, no third-party tracking, and no account requirement. It fetches only the public data you ask for.

No ads, no tracking

No Firebase Analytics, no behavioral telemetry, no third-party trackers, and no advertising identifiers — verified in the app's privacy manifests.

Computed on device

Box plots, survival curves, volcano plots, and heatmaps are calculated locally. The network is used only to fetch the public datasets you request.

Yours to move

Bookmarks and pinned cohorts stay private to you and export/import as portable files — carry your work between devices, no cloud lock-in.

Bring the genome with you.

A serious genomics bench for researchers — free of ads, accounts, and tracking. Available for iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Android.
